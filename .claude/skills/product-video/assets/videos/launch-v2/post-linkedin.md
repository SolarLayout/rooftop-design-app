The form of a product launch as OpenAI, Anthropic, Vercel and Stripe post one (the owner, 2026-10-14): a hook that
names the product and what it does in one full sentence, before LinkedIn's "…see more"; a body that a reader scans,

availability; and the links in the first comment. Plain words throughout (`references/posts.md`): full sentences, no

## Version A (recommended), about 1,200 characters

Introducing SolarLayout Rooftop, an app for designing rooftop PV systems in the browser.

It is free, and no account is needed.

You start by finding the roof by its coordinates or its address. After that, Rooftop does the following:

• It detects the building's footprint from the map, and you can drag the corners.
• It shows the roof in 3D on satellite imagery, with the modules laid out clear of the parapet and the walkways.
• It works out the shade on the modules over a year.
• It chooses an inverter for the roof and compares the stringing options by DC/AC ratio, clipping and energy.
• It simulates a typical year hour by hour and lists the losses and the P50, P75 and P90 values.
• It gives you the report, the single-line diagram, the bill of materials, the cable schedule, and DXF and KMZ files.
• It can present the design to a client as a tour in 3D.

The roof in the video is in Pune and has an area of about 5,300 m². The design is 705 kWp on four inverters, and it
produces 1,175 MWh in the first year.

Rooftop works on a phone, a tablet or a computer, and your designs are saved on your device.

The link is in the first comment.
