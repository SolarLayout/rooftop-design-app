SolarLayout Rooftop is a tool for designing rooftop PV systems, and it runs in the browser.

You find the roof on the map by its coordinates or its address, and the app detects the building's footprint. The roof
opens in 3D with the modules laid out on the satellite imagery. The app works out the shade on every module for the
whole year, chooses an inverter for the roof, compares the stringing options, and simulates every hour of a typical
year.

For the handover you get the report, the single-line diagram, the bill of materials, the cable schedule, and DXF and
KMZ files. You can also present the design to a client as a tour of the roof in 3D.

It is free, with no account needed, and it works on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

**[Open SolarLayout Rooftop](https://rooftop.solarlayout.app)**

The documentation is at https://solarlayout.app/docs/rooftop.

The SolarLayout team
