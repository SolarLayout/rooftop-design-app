## Version A (recommended), about 1,000 characters

Today we are launching SolarLayout Rooftop. It is a tool for designing rooftop PV systems, and it runs in the browser.

You start by entering the coordinates of the roof or searching for an address. When you click the building, its footprint
is detected from the map, and you can drag the corners if you need to. When you build the roof, it appears in 3D with the
modules already laid out, on the satellite imagery.

From there, the shading on every module is calculated for the whole year, the strings are sized for the inverter you
choose, and every hour of a typical year is simulated with each loss listed and the P50, P75 and P90 values. For the
handover you get the report, the single-line diagram, the bill of materials, the cable schedule, and DXF and KMZ files.

The roof in the video is a real roof of about 5,000 square metres in Pune. The design came to 649 kWp and 1,155 MWh a
year, and it took under two minutes from the map to the result.

It is free, there is no account to create, and it works on a phone, a tablet or a computer. Your designs stay on your
device.

The link is in the first comment.
