## Version A (recommended), about 800 characters

The Sun lens of SolarLayout Rooftop shows where the sun stands over your roof, and what it shades.

You can play the day and watch the shadows move across the roof hour by hour, with the sun's height and bearing at each
hour. The engine's own shadow outlines can be drawn over the view, so you can check what you see against what is
calculated. You can look at the solstices, the equinox, or any date you choose. The shade map colours every module by its
shading over the whole year, and the losses are given as near, electrical and far shading.

The roof in the video is a real roof in Pune, shown on the satellite imagery.

It is free, with no account needed, and it runs in the browser. The link is in the first comment.
