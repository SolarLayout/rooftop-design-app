V2 · 8 · Performance

Performance answers one question at first sight: how much energy, and how good the system is. The number with what it depends on beside it (the option, the weather), its likely and conservative values, and three figures. The page fits the screen; nothing scrolls.

Everything else waits under it, one view at a time, in four tabs: By month, Over 25 years, Where the energy goes, Assumptions; the tab opened last is remembered. Where the energy goes shows eleven plain steps in two columns, before the modules and in the system; the steps the user may change are fields. The engine's 21 steps are behind All 21 steps.
