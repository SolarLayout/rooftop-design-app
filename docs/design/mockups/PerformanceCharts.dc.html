The card has Chart and Table as tabs in its head (decision 9, the board “Tabs”). The bars are the run's monthly_mwh on one axis, the extremes labelled: March 68.4, July 31.5. Under the pointer the bar is the target and lifts (brightness 1.14, the hover of every control); its tooltip stands beside its top, the value first: “68.4 MWh · 191 kWh/kWp”, then “Mar”. The month's kWh/kWp is its MWh × 1,000 over the DC capacity, 357.28 kWp. The extreme's label gives way to the tooltip while it is shown.

The table is the same numbers (components.md “Table”): Month in Geist 500, MWh and kWh/kWp in mono 13 at the right, 12 rows of 44, and the year as a last row in Geist 600 over a line: 617.8 MWh and 1,729 kWh/kWp, the run's energy and specific yield. The head says what option it is of.

The tooltip is never the only place: the table holds every value (decision 18, guidelines “Charts”). On touch a tap on a bar shows it.

Degradation is a field of the document, 0 to 3 %/yr (inventory 1.7), in the head of the card. At 0.70 the energy of year 1 is unchanged, 617.8 MWh; year 25 is 522.0 in place of 547.8, and the total 14,216 MWh in place of 14,554. The tile of the 25 years follows.

The answer comes in about a quarter of a second: the degradation touches no hour, so the figures after the year are worked out from the year's energy without simulating again (decision 5). The line is fitted to the new extremes, so it falls across the same height; the numbers say how much.
