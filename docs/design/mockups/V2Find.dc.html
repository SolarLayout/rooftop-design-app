V2 · 4 · Find

Find has one job: an outline of the roof, then Build. It takes the Studio's work column: the instruction at the head, what to do under it, the primary at the foot. The instruction follows the step, never the tool in hand.

The first step is a search field, the first thing in the column, with its two other ways under it: Use my location and Enter the roof by hand. Examples show in the field's list when it is empty. No cards, no Three steps.

Outlining: the tools with their words, Undo and Clear with theirs. Once the outline closes, the column says what the roof is and asks what is left: the obstacles and the roof type, a question with a default. Build this roof stands pinned at the foot from then on, at every size. The roof shows its own handles; no card of tips.
