V2 · 6 · The Strings step

The Strings step leads with what the user decides: the options, best first. Each says why it is there in two or three words. The chosen one says it fits; its proof (the voltages at the coldest and hottest hours) is behind Why.

The rules the options are made by move behind How the options are made, in three plain lines. Add your own option stands under the options, in view. The inverter, the system voltage, the MPPT loading and the sizing temperatures are lines of facts that open, each saying who set it.

The strings are drawn on the roof in the colour of their MPPT. The legend stands at a fixed place, at the stage's foot on desktop and tablet, at the sheet's head on phone, never over the roof.
