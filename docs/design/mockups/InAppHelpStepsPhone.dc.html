How-tos with pictures

Help for Find's tools, as you asked: numbered steps, each with a picture of the app and callouts on it, in the same help sheet; built so that a short clip can take a picture's place later. Drawn by a prototype in the app (one topic, "Move or turn the roof", and its two ways in).

1 · Where a how-to opens

  1. 1Beside the panel's title, a ? opens the how-to of what the hand can do now: with the roof open, the outline tool chosen (Corners, Rectangle, Detect building); once it is closed, the obstacle tool chosen (Box, Tank, Wall, Building); with the roof or an obstacle picked, how to move, turn or delete it.
  2. 2A tip gets Show me how: the same how-to, at the moment its move becomes possible. Got it stays as it is.
  3. 3For you to decide: the title has room for one ?. Proposed: the disc takes the place of the toolbar's "Show the tips" ?, and each how-to ends with "Show the tips on the map again".

2 · A how-to: numbered steps, each with its picture

  1. 1The sheet as for every topic: where it was opened (Tool · Find), the title and the summary.
  2. 2The steps, numbered in the disc's orange, a line joining them; each a short title, what to do, and a sentence or two in the words of the hand in use.
  3. 3A picture of the app itself, cut to the part that matters. What to act on is ringed in the accent and glows; numbered callouts sit on it.
  4. 4The callouts' words under the picture, by their numbers: they are read without zooming into it.

3 · The hand in use, the moment caught, what follows

  1. 1Words for the hand in use: click, drag and keys with a mouse; press, one finger and two on touch. A key or a gesture in the chip of the tips.
  2. 2Up to three callouts on a picture; a ring only round the thing to act on.
  3. 3The picture holds the moment: caught mid-drag or mid-turn, with how far or how much as the app writes it beside the roof.
  4. 4Then: what follows (nothing changes in the Studio until Build this roof), how to take a step back, and the related topics.

4 · The pictures

  • Taken from the app by a script, on the demo roof at twice the screen's pixels, so that they show the screen as it is; taken again when it changes (bun help:pictures), and the change is seen in the pull request.
  • On a plain ground in place of the satellite imagery: no licence to clear for the imagery, nothing behind the roof to distract, a small file (60 kB a picture).
  • The callouts are drawn by the sheet over the picture, not into it: crisp at every size, and kept as numbers in the topic. The gate checks that every picture is there, has words for a screen reader, and its callouts fall inside it.
  • On phone and tablet a picture pressed opens at the width of the screen, and pinches; Back or × returns to the step.

5 · Later: a short clip in the same frame

0:09
  1. 1Later, a step may show a short clip in the same frame: its first frame with a play button and its length; it plays in place, muted, never by itself, and loops. The step's words stay as its caption, the callouts as the clip's marks.
  2. 2A picture first, always; a clip only where a gesture is hard to see still (turning, a drag that snaps). Nothing in this work waits for clips.

6 · The how-tos of Find (about ten topics, find.tool.…)

  • Outline by its corners (Corners): click each corner, close on the first or with Enter; drag a corner, add one on an edge, remove one; Square the corners.
  • Draw a rectangle (Rectangle): drag across the roof, then turn it by its handle.
  • Detect the building (Detect building): click inside it, then accept the outline proposed or adjust it.
  • Move or turn the roof: the sample on this board.
  • A box, a tank, a wall, a building beside the roof: how each is drawn, and its height asked right after.
  • Move, turn or delete an obstacle; its height in the list.
  • Undo, redo and clear, and what Build this roof keeps.
  • Each is written as these: numbered steps, a picture for each, pointer and touch in their own words; the sentences checked against the code.
