V2 · 9 · Handover and Home

Handover lists the files by who they are for: the client, the utility, the installer, and you. Each says what it is and its size; the badge is its format alone. The preview takes the rest of the width. Download all is the one primary; each file has its own Download. Export and Save a copy are gone: the project file is a file of the list.

Home is a start screen. One title, one line of what the app does, then the search, the largest thing on the page, with the other ways in under it. When there are designs on this device they come first, under the search, which stays at the top as the page scrolls; the demo follows. The design card is the same on Home and in the project menu.
