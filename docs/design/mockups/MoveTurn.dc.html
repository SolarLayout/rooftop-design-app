Move and turn the roof and its obstacles

Once the roof is outlined (by corners, a rectangle or Detect building) it can be picked up whole, moved and turned, on Find's map, against the imagery: the Roof lens's Edit leads there. In the Studio's stage the roof is the origin of everything, and turning it there would turn the sun, the rows and the energy with nothing to check it against. Obstacles move and turn on the map as they already do on the Studio's roof. The tips are small cards at the map's edge, one at a time, shown when a move becomes possible, never over what is moved: Got it closes a tip for good on this device, and it also goes by itself once the move it explains is done; the ? of the toolbar shows the tips again. Desktop: a click picks, a drag moves, the arrow keys nudge; the cursor says what a drag does.

  2. 2Dragged, the roof and its obstacles follow; the label says how far: “1.6 m E · 0.6 m S”. The edges, the area and the obstacles keep their measures; one step of Undo puts it back. The arrow keys nudge it 0.1 m, 1 m with Shift.
  3. 3The round handle on its stem, 44 above the middle of the north edge, turns it about its middle (next frame).

2 · Turn the roof

  1. 1The handle is held: its ring thicker, the cursor a closed hand.
  2. 2The label follows: the turn and where the roof now faces, “6.0° · faces 186° S”. Near north the turn snaps within 3°, as the rectangle does; the panel's Faces says the same.
  3. 3It turns about its middle, the obstacles with it; let go, the turn is kept, one step of Undo. What follows is said where it matters: the array's azimuth follows the roof's edge closest to the equator, so the rows turn with it.
