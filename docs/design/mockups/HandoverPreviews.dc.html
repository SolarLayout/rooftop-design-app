The SVG follows the engine's geometry (plots.py, draw_sld): a box per inverter at the middle, 6 blocks of MPPTs at the left, each in the series colour of its MPPT as the Strings lens colours them, the words “4 × 29 mod” in white or accent-ink as the colour allows; a line to the inverter with “MPPT n · DC” over it; from the inverter “AC” to the ACDB, the meter “kWh” with “Net meter”, and “Grid / LT panel”. The inverter says “INV-1” and “275 kW · 6 MPPT”. Every MPPT of the demo has strings, so no “not used” is drawn; an MPPT without strings would say “MPPT n · not used” in its line, as the engine does.

Under the pointer the block of MPPT 2 lifts (brightness 1.14) and shows the tooltip of a value beside it, 8 to its right, as the tooltip of a chart stands beside its marker, so that no other block is hidden: its strings, “S5 · S6 · S7 · S8”, then its load from mppt_loads, “Imp 54.3 of 65.0 A”. The same comes with the keyboard focus and on a tap. A list for screen readers says every block.

The PNG is the engine's own drawing on A4 (2338 × 1654), held equal to this by the counts and the words, never by pixels; the line under the drawing says so.

On phone the DC side is scaled to the width and the AC side runs down from the inverter, so that every word keeps its size: the drawing is laid out again, not shrunk.
