V2 · 3 · The Studio's first open

After Build this roof the Studio opens on the Roof step and says what happened: the array is on the roof, the energy is on its way. Then one instruction, Check the roof, and one primary, Continue to Array.

The work column has three weights. First the step: its instruction, its status, its primary at the foot. Then the facts: each section one line that opens. Then the details: the fields, inside a section the user opens. The column remembers what was opened.

What the app set is told apart from what the user set: each section and each field says Default, From the weather, Set for India, or Yours. A new user sees what to check, not 40 settings.

The result is one figure, the energy of year 1, at the top bar's right on desktop and phone, in a row under the stage on tablet. It opens the summary. The time leaves the stage: it belongs to Sun.
