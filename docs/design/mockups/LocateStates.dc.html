Rectangle

Drag a box across the roof; then turn it to the building by the handle above its top edge. The corners drag as in Corners
90.0 m40.0 mTurned 4°

The result is the same polygon as by corners: four corners, the edges in the panel, Faces from the long side. The turn snaps to north within 3°.

The loupe on touch

A corner is dragged with a finger: a loupe of 104 px shows twice the imagery above it, with a cross at the corner. It goes when the finger lifts
40.2 m

Handles are 22 px and take a touch of 44 by 44 around them. Tapping the first corner closes the outline; a tap on the map elsewhere places a corner.
