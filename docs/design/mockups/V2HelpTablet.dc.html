Choosing one

Press a card to use it. Nothing runs again: every option is computed already.

What a card says

Why it is there in two words, the modules per string × strings, the system's size, the energy in year 1. A tick: it fits. An exclamation mark: its DC/AC ratio is outside the target.

How the options are made

  1. Each option keeps the DC/AC ratio between 1.10 and 1.30.
  2. An MPPT takes at most 4 strings.
  3. The least shaded strings are used first.
