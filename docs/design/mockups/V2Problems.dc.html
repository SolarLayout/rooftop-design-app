V2 · 5 · Problems

A problem has one shape everywhere: what happened, as a title; why, in one sentence; what to do, as a button; the technical detail behind Details. The cause comes before the consequence. No word of the machine in the first two lines: not HTTP, not the engine, not a key.

It has one place. A problem of the result shows on the result in the top bar. A problem of a step shows at the head of that step's column, and its first remedy becomes the step's primary. Something done for the user shows once, as a toast with Undo. A refused file stays in its dialog. The last good result stays on screen, marked.

A note to review is fixed where it is read: the field is in the note.
