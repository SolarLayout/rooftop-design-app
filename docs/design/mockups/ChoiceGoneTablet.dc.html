A choice of stringing that no longer fits

The note, a toast that stays until it is closed: “The stringing you chose, 18 in series × 84 strings on 7 inverters, no longer fits this design. Option A, Recommended, is used instead: 23 in series × 67 strings on 7 inverters.” Choose another leads to the options of the Strings lens.

When: a run lands and the design's chosen option is not among its options (another module, inverter, voltage or roof changed them), or a design is opened whose choice no longer fits. The design takes the option shown, as part of the edit that changed it: nothing to undo, nothing run again; undo gives the edit and its choice back. Nothing is said for a choice that still fits, nor twice for the same. Tablet: the same, across the width.
