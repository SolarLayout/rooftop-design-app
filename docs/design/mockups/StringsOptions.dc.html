Add your own opens in place of the dashed button: three fields prefilled with the chosen option, 29 × 22 × 1, each with its bounds under it (decision 9): the series by the range, 14 to 29; the strings by what the roof holds of that length, 23 of 29 (strings_by_series of the engine, A's available_strings); the inverters at least one string each. The focus goes to Series. Add commits the option and chooses it; Cancel closes the fields and changes nothing.

Your own chosen: the sixth card, F, with the caption Your own and the engine's numbers of 26 × 24 × 1: 349.44 kWp, DC/AC 1.27, 607.7 MWh, a tick. It is selected, A no longer; under it Change, which opens the same fields with its numbers, and Remove, which takes it out and chooses the run's first option (decision 9). The bar's marker moves to 26 and the verdict is your own's, in the engine's words.

There is one option of your own at most, so Add your own is not offered while there is one. Choosing a card asks nothing of the engine: every option carries its results (decision 3).

The verdict under the bar is the engine's detail, word for word, with its “inverter(s)”; the approved board wrote it shorter by hand.

At 1000 V the range is 14 to 19 and the engine answers four options, all below the DC/AC target: their captions come from their data (decision 11): the note, and the inverters only where there are more than one, so A and B read “Below target, 2 inverters”, C and D “Below target”. None is recommended; the run's first, A, is chosen.

Your own of 29 in series no longer fits. The engine does not simulate it and says why (custom_refused, decision 8): “29 in series does not fit at 1000 V”, “At most 19 fit: Voc at 11.9 °C is 1,480 V, over the limit of 1000 V. Change your option or remove it.” The card keeps the numbers you gave, a note mark, no kWp, no DC/AC and no energy, since what cannot be built has none; it cannot be chosen. Change and Remove stand under it. Its letter is the one after the run's options.

At 600 V no length fits. The bar has no band: the engine's two ends stand as ticks on the scale of lengths, the Voc limit, 11, below the MPPT minimum, 14, so the lower is at the left. The verdict is the note of no_options, its title and its message; there are no options and no Add your own, and a line says what can change it.

The track's scale: the range takes the middle 44 % of the track, as the approved board draws 14 to 29, so the labels of the ends never meet; with no band, the two ends take the same places, the lower one first.
