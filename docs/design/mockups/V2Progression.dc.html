V2 · 2 · The progression

One order of work, Roof to Handover, shown as one row of five steps. It takes the place of the switch of workspaces and the rail of lenses. Each step shows its state: done, needs you, or still to do. Every step stays a link: the row shows the order, it does not lock it.

Each step has one primary action, where the eye ends its path: at the foot of the work column on Find and in the Studio, at the head of the page on Performance and Handover. It leads to the next step. Export leaves the top bar: Download all is Handover's primary. Present moves to the head of Performance and Handover.

The design's result has one place on desktop: the energy of year 1 at the top bar's right, with what waits for review. The vitals bar over the roof goes (board 3 draws the result on tablet and phone). On desktop the work column can be hidden, to give the roof or the map the whole width: Hide panel at its head, ⌘ \ from anywhere. The primary stays, at the stage's foot, and the result stays in the top bar. The column is open at a design's first open, comes back when a step needs the user or an object is selected, and is remembered. Sun is a view of the stage, on every step, not a step between Array and Strings. Find is the first part of Roof: there is no roof to check before it is outlined.
