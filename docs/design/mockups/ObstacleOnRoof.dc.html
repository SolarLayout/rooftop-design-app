Obstacle edited on the roof

The obstacles of the Roof lens are edited on the 3D roof as well as in the list: selected by a press, moved by a drag, sized, turned and raised by handles; added by kind, with nothing preselected. One selection for the roof and the list.

Selected on the roof

The desktop stage about the stair room, selected: the accent outline, a handle at each corner of its top, the turn handle on its stem off the north edge, the height handle on a vertical stem from the middle of its top
Stair room · selected
5.0 × 4.0 m · 3.0 m high · drag to move

A press on the roof, an obstacle, a kept-clear area or a module selects it; hover is a lighter outline, on pointer devices only; Escape clears. The list and the roof share one selection: the row opens in the list, the callout stands on the roof. The parapet is not selectable.

Dragged

Mid-drag: the stair room follows the pointer on the roof plane, snapping to 0.1 m; its old place stays as a dashed ghost, the live position stands beside the pointer. The handles are away while it moves
East 27.5 m · North 18.0 m

The drop commits the edit: the layout follows at once (the kept-clear area moves with the stair room, the module count changes), the design after the pause. Undo (Cmd or Ctrl+Z) restores the place, the layout and the count; a drag is one edit. The obstacle stays inside the roof.

Each handle changes one thing: a corner the length and the width (the opposite corner stays), the turn handle the rotation (it snaps to 0° and 90° within 3°), the height handle the height. The fields of the list do the same by number, and say the same while a handle is dragged. A tank has one handle, at its edge, for its diameter.
