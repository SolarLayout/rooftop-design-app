In-app help

A ? beside each section heading of the input panels opens help on what the section is, its choices and what they do, in the app's own words. The core is the help system of the solarlayout app (topics in MDX, each with a key; a typed registry checked by tests; a drawer with Back and related topics; the topic in the address); the look and the places are this app's. The disc and the drawer are drawn here by a prototype in the app itself; the words of the sample topics are checked against the engine. Desktop: the drawer stands beside the panel, the fields still there to change.

1 · A ? beside each section heading (three times the screen)

  1. 1Beside every section heading of the input panels, 6 after its words: a disc of the primary action, its orange, its light from above and its shadow, a ? in its ink. 16 on desktop in a press of 24; 20 on touch in a press of 44. It is there only where the section has a topic.
  2. 2Hover lifts it as the primary buttons lift; the focus ring is the app's; pressed it sinks. While its help is open it stays held in, ringed, so the eye goes back to what is being explained.
  3. 3A real button: "Help: System voltage", it says it opens a dialog and whether it is open. The heading's own glow is untouched.

2 · Hover tells, a click opens

  1. 1On desktop, the pointer over the disc shows the topic's summary, one or two lines, and "Click for more". Touch has no hover: a press opens the drawer.
  2. 2The summary is the topic's own (140 characters at most): the same words lead the drawer, so nothing is said twice in two ways.

3 · The help drawer, beside the panel (desktop)

  1. 1The drawer slides in from the right and stands against the inspector's edge, under the top bar, 400 wide: the section and its fields stay in view, and can still be changed while the help is read. It does not dim the screen; Escape, the × or the disc again close it.
  2. 2The title and the summary, as the tooltip said them.
  3. 3Where a section is one choice, its options, each with what it means; the user's own marked "Your choice". Nothing is chosen for the user (the system voltage is never preselected).
  4. 4The topic's body: short headings, plain words, a value in the user's own on-screen words in mono, a note in the note's colours, and links to other topics.
  5. 5Related topics as rows, each with its summary, so that what is behind a link is known before it is followed.
  6. 6The disc of the topic shown is held in.

4 · Following a topic, and back

  1. 1A related topic or a link followed opens in the drawer; Back (‹) returns, as many steps as were taken. A disc pressed starts afresh. The topic shown is in the address (?help=studio.section.modules-in-series), so a link to it can be shared and opens it.

5 · On Find, floating beside the panel

  1. 1On Find the headings of the panel have their discs too: Edges, Obstacles, Roof type (and Place and Shape when the roof is entered by hand).
  2. 2Find's panel floats, so the drawer floats beside it in the same way, ending with its content: the map stays as visible as it can.
  3. 3The same drawer everywhere: where it was opened, its title, its body, its related topics.
