Every assumption is a field in its chip (decisions 6 and 8), with the range of inventory 1.7: Uc 5 to 60 W/m²K, the rear shading and mismatch 0 to 50 %, the albedo 0.05 to 0.90, the sigmas 0 to 20 %, the levels 50 to 99, the CO₂ factor 0 to 2 t/MWh. Changed to 50, 90, 99, the lead tile's cells follow: P50 615.7, P90 582.3, P99 555.0 MWh, σ 4.24 % on the last.
