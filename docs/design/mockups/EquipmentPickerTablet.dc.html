Choose a module or an inverter

Replace on the module card and on the inverter card opens a picker over the app's own library of PVsyst files. A search over the maker, the model and the file's name, and range sliders to narrow the list: the module by its power, the inverter by its AC power and its count of MPPT. The list is sorted by power. A row is chosen, then used; the file is read by the engine as a dropped file is, so the card, the layout and the energy follow as they do today. A file of one's own is still taken, under the list.
