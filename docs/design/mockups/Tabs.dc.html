When tabs, when a segmented control

Tabs divide the content of one card or one panel into parts: two to six of them, named by what is in them. Every part is there at once; a tab shows one. A segmented control sets a value of the design or the view of the stage: 3D or Plan, Portrait or Landscape.

Keyboard and names

The keyboard stops at the row once, at the tab that is taken. The arrows left and right go to the neighbour and around the ends, Home and End to the first and the last; each takes the tab it goes to, and a tab that cannot be taken is passed over. Tab leads into the panel. To a screen reader: tablist, tab, tabpanel, the panel named by its tab.
