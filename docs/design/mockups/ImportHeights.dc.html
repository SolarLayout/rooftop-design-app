A DXF holds the roof's shape and nothing of where it is on Earth, and the place sets the sun, the shadows and the weather: so nothing is guessed (the desktop app put every DXF at its own default, Pune). The place is searched or pasted, in the words of Find, or typed; the same two fields as "Enter dimensions by hand". The API refuses a DXF without a place (site_missing) and the dialog never sends one without. A KMZ or KML needs no place: the block is not shown.

The largest closed polyline is the roof; other closed polylines and circles are obstacles, named by their layer: a circle a tank, a near-rectangle a box with its turn, a line a wall, anything else a building. A KMZ or KML gives its place and heights from the placemarks; a DXF gives neither, and its units and its place are asked. Open the roof opens the design in the Studio at the Roof lens. On phone and tablet the dialog is a bottom sheet.

Review, or Next, selects the first obstacle of the list to review and puts the focus in its height. A typed height commits as any field and takes the obstacle off the list; Next selects the following one; the note goes when the list is empty.
