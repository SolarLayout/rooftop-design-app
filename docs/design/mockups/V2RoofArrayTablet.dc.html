V2 · 7 · The Roof and Array steps, and the Sun view

Every field says what it is in plain words, the trade's term after it where there is one: Edge margin (setback), Walkway every 24 modules. Fields stand in groups of what belongs together, 8 apart inside a group and 24 between groups. The label is in primary text at the value's weight; the hint under it says who set the value or the usual one.

The module card names the module as a buyer does: model, maker, power, size, and Change. Its electrical figures are behind Details. Spacing says in one sentence why the rows stand where they do. Roof albedo has one home, Performance's assumptions; the roof's line here points to it.

The Sun view names its figures for what they are: Rows and obstacles, Strings, Horizon, Area kept clear. The time and its timeline belong to this view alone. Layers is one toggle, Imagery, in the view controls.
