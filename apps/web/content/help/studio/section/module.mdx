## What the card shows

The model, the maker and the rated power; the size, and the bifaciality of a bifacial module; Voc, Isc, the bypass diodes, and whether it is twin half-cell. Then the temperature coefficients of Voc, Vmp and Pmpp, with where the Voc coefficient comes from: `PAN file`, `one-diode model` or `entered`. When the engine has a note on the module, an exclamation mark stands beside the coefficients and the note follows: a Voc coefficient outside the usual range for crystalline silicon, or a file that gives no size of the module. The file's name stands at the foot.

## Change

`Change` opens `Choose a module`: the app's library of PAN files, with a search over the maker, the model and the file's name, and a filter by `Power`. `Use this module` reads the one chosen; a PAN file of your own can be chosen or dropped instead. A file that cannot be read leaves the module as it was and says why. A new module lays the roof out again, and the design runs again.

## What the module sets

Its size, with the <HelpLink k="studio.section.mounting">mounting</HelpLink>, sets the footprint of every position. Its voltages, its temperature coefficients and its highest system voltage set the lengths of string that fit. Its Imp and Isc set how many strings an MPPT can take. Its rated power sets the kWp and the DC/AC of each option.

## The Voc coefficient

The card shows the coefficient the PAN file states, or the one-diode model's when it states none. The strings are sized with the one-diode model's unless you enter a coefficient: when the datasheet gives one you trust, enter it under `Sizing temperatures` in the Strings lens.
