## On the timeline

At the right of the timeline of the Sun view, the sun at the hour shown: its height above the horizon in degrees, and the direction it comes from, clockwise from north, with the point of the compass in words, as `Sun 18.0° high, from 237° (south-west)`. While it is below the horizon, `The sun is down`.

## The hour

The timeline names the hour, in solar time: noon is when the sun stands highest, as in the engine. Move it with the timeline's handle, with play, or with the `,` and `.` keys, an hour at a time. On a day the sun rises and sets, the hour stays between sunrise and sunset. The time belongs to the Sun view: the steps draw the design day's noon.
