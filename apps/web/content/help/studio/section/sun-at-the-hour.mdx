## The two tiles

`Height` is the sun's elevation above the horizon, in degrees. `Bearing` says where it stands, measured from whichever of south and north is nearer, with the side it is on: east or west of south, or of north. Straight along a compass line it reads `due south`, `due north`, `due east` or `due west`.

## The hour

The heading names the hour, in solar time: noon is when the sun stands highest, as in the engine. Move the hour with the timeline on the stage, or with the `,` and `.` keys, an hour at a time. On a day the sun rises and sets, the hour stays between sunrise and sunset.

## When there are no tiles

`The sun is down` while it is below the horizon at that hour. `The sun's day is on its way` while the sun of the day is being worked out, and `The sun was not fetched` if that failed.
