## What to set

Keep the app's values unless the racking fixes them. On `Tilted rows`:

- `Tilt`: the angle of the modules from flat. Its hint says `Default · low for wind on a flat roof`.
- `Orientation`: `Portrait` puts the module's long side up the slope, `Landscape` its short side.
- `Modules in tilt`: how many modules stand one above another in a row. Its hint says `Default · one module up each row`.
- `Clearance under the lower edge`: the height of the modules' lower edge above the roof.

The fields follow the roof type. `East-west` has `East-west tilt` (`Each side, away from the ridge`), `Ridge gap`, the space between the two sides at the ridge, and `Clearance under the lower edge`. Each side holds one module, its short side up the slope. `Sloped` has `Orientation` and `Modules in tilt`: `Tilt` reads the roof's pitch from the Roof step, and the modules lie a small fixed height above the roof.

## What it changes

The app lays out the roof again, then runs the design.

- A steeper tilt or more modules in tilt makes a row taller. Its shadow grows, and rows spaced by the shade-free hours stand further apart.
- The orientation changes the size of each position, and so how many fit along a row.
- The app works out the shadows of obstacles and the parapet at the height of the lower edge: a higher edge keeps less of the roof free of modules.
- The tilt changes the sunlight on the modules, and so the energy.

Under each field stands who set it: `Default`, or `Yours · default` with the app's value and `Reset`, which gives it back.

## When to change it

When the racking has a set tilt or height, or when the other orientation fits more modules on the roof.
