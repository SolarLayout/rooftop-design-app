Where modules may not go: the strip at the edges, the walkways, and the space round obstacles.

## What to set

Measure the parapet on site: it shades the first row. Keep the other values unless the site's rules ask for more.

- `Parapet height`: the low wall along the roof's edge. If there is none, type 0. Its hint says `Default · measure it on site`.
- `Edge margin (setback)`: the strip along every edge kept free of modules, `usually 0.5 to 1.5 m`. The fire code may set it.
- `Walkway width` and `Walkway every`: a path across the rows, `room to clean and repair the modules`. On `Tilted rows`, `Walkway every` is `counted in modules along a row`; on `East-west` and `Sloped` roofs the path runs between the rows and is `counted in rows`. At 0, there is no walkway.
- `Clearance`: the space `kept free on every side` of each obstacle.

On a `Sloped` roof, `Roof pitch` and `Direction (azimuth)` follow: the slope of the roof and the way it faces, clockwise from north. The modules lie on the roof at that pitch and face that way.

The line under the fields, `Roof albedo` with its value and `in Performance, Assumptions`, says how much light the roof reflects. Press it to set it in Performance.

## What it changes

Each value moves where modules may go. The app lays out the roof again, then runs the design.

- The edge margin draws the area modules may fill in from every edge.
- The parapet stands along every edge like a wall. The area its shadow crosses in the shade-free hours stays free of modules, unless `Allow shaded positions` is on in Spacing. Its shade counts in the energy over the whole year.
- The clearance widens each obstacle on every side.

Under each field stands who set it: `Default`, or `Yours · default` with the app's value and `Reset`, which gives it back. Undo takes either back.

## When to change it

When the site has no parapet, when its code asks for a wider margin, or when the roof needs a walkway more often.
