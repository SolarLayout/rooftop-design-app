## The fields

- `Shade-free window`: its start and its end, in solar time, by the quarter hour. The start is noon at the latest and the end noon at the earliest.
- `Design day`: `Winter solstice` (of the site's hemisphere), `21 Jun` or `21 Mar`.
- `Allow shaded positions`: off, positions shaded inside the window stay empty; on, they are used too.

## What it sets

The engine follows the sun through the window on the design day, every quarter hour.

- Obstacles and parapets: their shadows across the window, at the height of the modules' lower edge, are kept free of modules unless shaded positions are allowed. This applies on every roof type.
- Row pitch on tilted rows, when the engine decides it: rows are set far enough apart that one row does not shade the next at the sun's lowest profile angle in the window.
- East-west units: they stand side by side with no gap unless the window needs one; then the engine adds the gap that keeps them shade-free. A layout note gives the gap.

## When to change it

Widening the window can only add space between rows and keep more of the roof clear around obstacles; narrowing it can fit more modules, and accepts shade outside the window. The window shapes the layout only: the energy run still works out the shade on every module for every hour of the weather year.

## Who set it

Closed, the section is one line: the window in solar time and the design day, with who set them. The window, the design day and `Allow shaded positions` each say `Default`, or `Yours` with the default and `Reset`, which gives the default back.
