## The three switches

- `Sun path`: the arc the sun follows across the sky on the day shown. On by default.
- `Shade-free window`: the part of the arc inside the shade-free window, drawn thicker. Its line gives the window's hours. On by default. It is dimmed and cannot be turned while the sun path is off.
- `Calculated shadow outlines`: the shadows of the parapet and the obstacles on the roof surface at the day and hour shown, as the engine works them out, dashed over the shadows the view renders. Off by default.

## How to read the outlines

Where the dashed outlines and the rendered shadows agree, the view is true. The outlines are asked for once the hour stays still for a moment, and are shown only while they are of the hour on screen.

The view, the outlines and the shade-free window that spaces the rows place the sun by solar time on the day shown. The year's shading places it at every hour of the weather, from its exact position. The two differ by a fraction of a degree: the view is a close guide, and the year is counted exactly.

## What they change

Nothing of the design. The switches only change what is drawn. The hours of the shade-free window are set in the Array lens.
