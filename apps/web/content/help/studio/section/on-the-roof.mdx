## What to set

Turn on what the Sun view draws over the roof. Unless you are checking the shade, keep the first two on and the other two off.

- `Sun path`: the arc the sun follows across the sky on the day shown.
- `Shade-free hours`: the part of the arc inside the shade-free hours, drawn thicker. Its line gives the hours. It is dimmed while `Sun path` is off.
- `Shadow outlines`: the shadows of the parapet and the obstacles at the day and time shown, as the app computes them, dashed over the shadows the view draws.
- `Shade map on the modules`: each module coloured by its loss to shade over the year, with the map's steps under the switches. See <HelpLink k="studio.section.shade-map">Shade map</HelpLink>.

## What it changes

Only what is drawn. The layout, the strings and the energy stay as they are. The shade-free hours are set in the Array step, in <HelpLink k="studio.section.shade-free-hours">Shade-free hours</HelpLink>.

## When to change it

Turn on `Shadow outlines` to check the view: where the dashed outlines and the drawn shadows agree, the view is true. The outlines show a moment after the time stops moving. Turn on the shade map to see which modules lose most to shade.
