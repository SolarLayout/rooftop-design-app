## Reading it

At the right of the Sun view's timeline, on a desktop, a line says where the sun stands at the time shown: its height above the horizon in degrees, and the direction it comes from, in degrees clockwise from north with the point of the compass, as `Sun 18.0° high, from 237° (south-west)`. While the sun is below the horizon, it says `The sun is down`. The sun's marker on the stage gives the same height, with the direction from south or north.

## The day

`Day` chooses the day whose sun, path and shadows the view shows: `21 Dec`, `21 Mar`, `21 Jun`, or `Any date`, which opens a `Day` field and a `Month` list. It changes what is seen, not the design. The line under it names the <HelpLink k="studio.section.design-day">design day</HelpLink>, as `Design day: 21 December, the winter solstice in this hemisphere`; choose the design day's date and the view follows the design day when it changes.

## The time

The timeline gives the time in solar time: noon is when the sun stands highest. Drag its handle, or press play to run through the day. The time stays between sunrise and sunset; on a day the sun does not set or does not rise, it runs from midnight to midnight. The thick part of the track is the shade-free hours.

## Keys

- `,` and `.`: an hour back or forward.
- `[` and `]`: a day back or forward.
- `←` and `→` on the timeline: a quarter hour.

The year's shading does not use this time: the app works it out from the sun's position at each hour of the weather.
