## How it limits a string

A string's open-circuit voltage is highest when its modules are coldest. At the coldest sizing temperature, the voltage of the whole string must stay at or under the lowest of three limits: the system voltage, the inverter's highest DC input voltage, and the module's highest system voltage from its file. That sets the most modules a string can hold.

## Nothing is chosen for you

The system voltage depends on the site and its equipment, so it is yours to decide. Until you choose, nothing is sized: `Modules in series`, the stringing options and the recommendation, the energy, PR and yield, the single-line diagram, the bill of materials and the exports wait, and say so.

<Callout>The bill of materials names the DC cable by the voltage you choose.</Callout>
