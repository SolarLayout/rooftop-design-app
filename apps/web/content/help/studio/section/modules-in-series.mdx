## Where it is

Once the strings are sized, the chosen option says that its length fits, and `Why` under it opens the lengths that fit at your system voltage: the fewest, `14 at least`, set by the `MPPT minimum`, and the most, `29 at most`, set by the `Voc limit` or the `MPPT maximum`, whichever is tighter, with a marker at the chosen length. Where no length fits, the bar stands under the problem at the head of the step, and says `None fit`.

## How the ends are set

- Shortest: with the cell at its hottest (the highest ambient plus the cell rise), the string's MPP voltage must stay at or above the inverter's MPPT minimum.
- `Voc limit`: with the cell at the lowest ambient, the string's open-circuit voltage must stay at or under the lowest of the system voltage, the inverter's highest DC voltage and the module's highest system voltage (from its file, or `1000 V` when the file gives none).
- `MPPT maximum`: with the cell at the lowest ambient, the string's MPP voltage must stay at or under the inverter's MPPT maximum.

The temperatures and the Voc coefficient are under `Sizing temperatures`. The open-circuit voltage at the lowest ambient comes from the module's one-diode model, and the coefficient beside the field is that model's; a Voc coefficient you enter from the datasheet takes its place.

## The proof

Behind `Why`, two plain lines. On the coldest morning, at the lowest ambient: the string's open-circuit voltage against the lowest of the system voltage, the inverter's highest DC voltage and the module's highest system voltage. On the hottest afternoon, with the cell at its hottest: the string's MPP voltage against the inverter's MPPT minimum. A line carries a tick on the chosen option when it is in its DC/AC target and every MPPT is within its current limit; otherwise the line carries an exclamation mark, and the engine's words on what it misses follow the proof.

## When no length fits

When the shortest length needed is longer than the longest allowed, the heading says `None fit`, the verdict under the bar gives both numbers, and there are no options. The step says it at its head: `No string length fits at` the voltage, what the inverter needs and what a string holds. Its buttons are the ways out: `Choose another inverter` and `Change the sizing temperatures`, and, where a higher voltage the equipment takes would fit a string, `Use` that voltage as the step's main button, to be pressed only where the site's code allows it. Until one length fits, no option can be built, your own included.
