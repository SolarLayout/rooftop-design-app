## The bar

It appears once the strings are sized. The band holds the lengths that fit, and the words beside the heading say which. The left end is the shortest length, set by the `MPPT minimum`. The right end is the longest, set by the `Voc limit` or the `MPPT maximum`, whichever is tighter. The marker is the length of the chosen option.

## How the ends are set

- Shortest: with the cell at its hottest (the highest ambient plus the cell rise), the string's MPP voltage must stay at or above the inverter's MPPT minimum.
- `Voc limit`: with the cell at the lowest ambient, the string's open-circuit voltage must stay at or under the lowest of the system voltage, the inverter's highest DC voltage and the module's highest system voltage (from its file, or `1000 V` when the file gives none).
- `MPPT maximum`: with the cell at the lowest ambient, the string's MPP voltage must stay at or under the inverter's MPPT maximum.

The temperatures and the Voc coefficient are under `Sizing temperatures`. The open-circuit voltage at the lowest ambient comes from the module's one-diode model, and the coefficient beside the field is that model's; a Voc coefficient you enter from the datasheet takes its place.

## The verdict

Under the bar stands the engine's verdict on the chosen option: its MPP voltage at STC, the voltages at both temperatures against their limits, its strings, inverters and DC/AC. It carries a tick when the option is in its DC/AC target and every MPPT is within its current limit, and its first line says the length fits. Otherwise it carries an exclamation mark, its first line names the length without saying it fits, and the rest says what it misses.

## When no length fits

When the shortest length needed is longer than the longest allowed, the heading says `None fit`, the verdict gives both numbers, and there are no options. Change the system voltage, the inverter or the sizing temperatures: until one length fits, no option can be built, your own included.
