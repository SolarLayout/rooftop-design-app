## What to set

Keep the winter solstice, the shortest day: the sun stands lowest and the shadows are longest, so rows spaced for it stand furthest apart. `Design day`, in the Array step's Spacing, offers the winter solstice of the roof's side of the equator, its summer solstice and `21 Mar`: `21 Dec`, `21 Jun` and `21 Mar` in the north, `21 Jun`, `21 Dec` and `21 Mar` in the south. Its hint names the day with the longest shadows, as `The day the rows are spaced for. 21 Dec has the longest shadows.`

## What it changes

With the <HelpLink k="studio.section.shade-free-hours">shade-free hours</HelpLink>, the design day sets the row spacing on `Tilted rows`, the gap between `East-west` units, and the area round obstacles and the parapet kept free of modules. The app lays out the roof again, then runs the design. The Sun view shows the design day unless another day is chosen there (<HelpLink k="studio.section.the-sun-at-the-time">The sun at the time</HelpLink>).

## When to change it

When the client takes some winter shade between the rows for more modules: a day nearer summer gives shorter shadows, so the rows stand closer.
