## The choices

`21 Dec`, `21 Mar` and `21 Jun` are the two solstices and the March equinox. `Any date` opens a `Day` field and a `Month` list; a day past the end of its month is taken as the month's last day. The year has no leap day, as the engine's sun has none.

## What it changes

The day sets the sun of the view: its light and shadows on the stage, its path, where it stands at the hour, and the engine's shadow outlines when they are on. The `[` and `]` keys step a day back or forward. The design itself does not change: the layout keeps the design day, and the shading figures are over the whole year.

## The design day

The line under the choices says which day is the design day. Unless the array has one of its own, it is the winter solstice of the roof's hemisphere. The design day is set with the shade-free window in the Array lens. Choose the date of the design day here and the view stays on the design day, even if the array's design day changes later.
