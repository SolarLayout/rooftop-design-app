## What the card says

The first line is the place of the roof, its latitude and longitude in degrees with five decimals. The next lines say how the roof was entered (`Outlined on satellite`, `Entered by hand` or `From a file`), how many corners and obstacles it has, and its size. A rectangle is given by its two sides, any other outline by its area. The size is followed by the direction the roof faces: the side of its longest edge that looks toward the equator. A roof read from a drawing also shows the name of the file.

## Changing the outline

`Edit it in Plan` turns the stage to the plan with the roof selected and a handle at each corner of the outline. Drag a corner and let go: the outline takes it, snapped as every drag of the stage is, as one change that undo takes back, and the roof is laid out again. The label beside the pointer gives the two edges the corner joins. An outline that would cross itself is refused, and the roof stays as it was. Find is for a new roof.

In the Roof step the section is `Outline`, one line: the place in degrees to two decimals, the roof's area and `Edit it in Plan`. The roof's dimensions are drawn on the stage in the Roof step alone, one label for each pair of opposite edges of the same length.
