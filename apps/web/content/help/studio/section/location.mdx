## What the card says

The first line is the place of the roof, its latitude and longitude in degrees with five decimals. The next lines say how the roof was entered (`Outlined on satellite`, `Entered by hand` or `From a file`), how many corners and obstacles it has, and its size. A rectangle is given by its two sides, any other outline by its area. The size is followed by the direction the roof faces: the side of its longest edge that looks toward the equator. A roof read from a drawing also shows the name of the file.

## Changing the outline

`Edit` opens Find with the outline of this design on the map, where it stands. On a phone the whole row leads there. Build the roof again and the design takes the new outline, its place, its obstacles and its roof type. Its trees, which the map does not show, are kept where they stand. The parapet, setback, walkways and the rest of the roof stay as they were, except that a roof made `Sloped` there loses its parapet and gets a pitch where it had none, as in the Roof step.
