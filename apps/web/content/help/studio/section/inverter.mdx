## What to set

Choose the inverter that will be installed. A new design starts with one the app chose for its roof, marked `Chosen for this roof`: of Waaree's on-grid inverters, the one that keeps the most modules in the DC/AC target on the fewest inverters, at the system voltage. When you change the roof, the array or the voltage, the app chooses again. Once you choose one, the row says `Yours`, and `Choose for this roof` under the card gives the choice back to the app.

1. Press `Change` on the inverter's card to open `Choose an inverter`, the app's library.
2. Search by maker, model, file name or power, or press `Filter` to narrow the list by `AC power`, `MPPT` and `Max DC`.
3. Choose a row, then press `Use this inverter`. For an inverter the library does not hold, drop its inverter file (OND) under the list, or press `Choose a file`; on a phone, press `Choose your own inverter file (OND)`.

The design's own inverter is tagged `In this design`. A file its maker calls preliminary is tagged `Preliminary`. A row rated under the system voltage says so: with it, the strings are sized to its rating, and the voltage stays as it is.

## What it changes

The inverter sets how the strings are sized. Its MPPT voltage window sets the shortest string and can set the longest. Its highest DC voltage is one of the limits on a string's voltage on the coldest morning. Its AC power sets each option's DC/AC ratio, and so how many inverters an option takes. Its MPPTs, its inputs and its current per MPPT set how many strings one inverter takes. A new inverter clears any current per MPPT you entered, and the design runs again.

## When to change it

When the installer's inverter is another. When no string length fits, the step offers `Choose another inverter`. When no option reaches the DC/AC target with your inverter, the step says why at its head and offers the inverter for this roof, such as `Use Waaree 3P-136K (75-136 kW)`, and `Choose another inverter`, which opens the library filtered to the AC power that suits the roof. When the card says `Current per MPPT not in the inverter file`, keep the inverter and type the datasheet's value in `Current per MPPT`, under `Inverter limits and cabling`.

A file that cannot be read leaves the inverter as it was and says why. A file larger than any inverter file is refused before it is sent.
