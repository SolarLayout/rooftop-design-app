## What to set

Keep `Row spacing from the shade-free hours` on: the app spaces the rows so that one does not shade the next in the shade-free hours of the design day. The sentence at the top gives the result, as `Rows stand 6.45 m apart so that they do not shade each other from 09:00 to 16:00 on 21 Dec.`

- `Row spacing from the shade-free hours`: the row spacing, or row pitch, is the distance on plan from the front edge of one row to the front edge of the next. While the switch is on, the line under it says `Off: type the spacing yourself`. Off, type `Row spacing` yourself: the line then gives the `Shade-free spacing` beside yours.
- `Shade-free hours` and `Design day`: the <HelpLink k="studio.section.shade-free-hours">hours</HelpLink> and the <HelpLink k="studio.section.design-day">day</HelpLink> the rows keep free of each other's shade.
- `Direction (azimuth)`: the way the rows face, clockwise from north. With `Follow the roof edge` on, they face square off the roof edge nearest the equator. Off, type the direction.
- `Allow shaded positions`: off, positions shaded in the shade-free hours stay empty. On, they hold modules too.

An `East-west` roof has no row spacing: its units stand side by side. A `Sloped` roof has neither row spacing nor direction: its rows follow one another up the roof and face the way it slopes.

## What it changes

The app lays out the roof again, then runs the design. Rows closer together fit more modules, and shade each other outside the shade-free hours; the energy counts that shade.

The app finds the sun's lowest profile angle in the shade-free hours, the sun's height seen square to the rows, and sets the rows far enough apart for it. It rounds the spacing up to the next five centimetres. A spacing typed under a row's depth plus a small gap is raised, and a note says so.

Under each field stands who set it: `Default`, or `Yours · default` with the app's value and `Reset`, which gives it back. A field with a switch says nothing while the app's value stands.

## When to change it

Type a spacing when the racking fixes it, or when the client takes some shade for more modules: the sentence then gives the shade-free spacing beside yours. Turn off `Follow the roof edge` when the rows must face another way than the roof edge.
