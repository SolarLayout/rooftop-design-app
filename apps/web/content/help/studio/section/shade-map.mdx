## What it shows

Each position of the layout takes the colour of its near shading over the year, as the design run worked it out: the direct light shaded by the rows in front, the parapet and the obstacles, and the part of the sky they hide from it. Every position is coloured, also those a stringing option does not use.

## The steps

The scale runs from zero to the first round value that holds the largest loss: one, two, five, ten, twenty, fifty or a hundred percent. It is cut into five equal steps, and the list gives each step's range and how many positions fall in it. The legend on the stage shows the same scale.

## Reading one module

Select a module, or tap it, for its position number, its row (or unit, on an east-west roof) and its near shading. With a chosen option, it also says the string it belongs to and that string's shading, or that the option does not use it.

## When it waits

The map follows the design run. While the run is not yet of the layout shown, it says so and colours nothing, so that no module takes another layout's value.
