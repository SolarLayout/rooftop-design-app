## Reading it

Turn on `Shade map on the modules` in On the roof. Each module takes the colour of its near shading over the year: the light that the rows in front, the parapet and the obstacles take from it, the part of the sky they hide included. The horizon is not in the map, as it takes the same from each module. All positions are coloured, including those the option does not use.

The list under the switches gives each step's range and how many positions fall in it. The scale runs from zero to the first round value that holds the largest loss: one, two, five, ten, twenty, fifty or a hundred percent. It is cut into five equal steps. The legend on the stage shows the same scale.

## One module

Select a module, or tap it, for its position, its row (its unit, on an east-west roof) and its near shading. With a chosen option, it adds the string the module belongs to and that string's shading, or says that the option does not use it.

## When it waits

The map follows the run. Until the run is of the layout shown, it says `Waits for the run` and colours nothing, so that no module shows another layout's value.
