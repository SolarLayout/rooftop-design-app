## What to set

Measure the roof on site, or take its measurements from a drawing. For a rectangle, choose `Rectangle` and type its length and width. For any other outline, choose `Corners`.

- `Length` and `Width`: in metres. The longer of the two is the long side, whichever field it is typed in.
- `The long side faces`: the direction the long side looks, `South`, `East`, `North` or `West`. South at first.
- `Corners`: `Type each corner in metres east and north of the place, in order. At least 3.` `Add a corner` adds a row; its bin removes one.

## What it changes

The app draws the roof about the <HelpLink k="find.section.place">place</HelpLink> as soon as the place and the shape are complete, and `Build this roof` says its area. A row of the corners with a blank is left out.

After that the roof is the same as one outlined on the map: choose its <HelpLink k="app.section.roof-type">roof type</HelpLink>, then build it. Add its obstacles in the Studio's Roof step.

## When to change it

Choose `Corners` when the roof is not a rectangle: an L, a notch, a corner cut off. Enter the roof by hand when the map has no imagery of it, or when its measurements are surer than the imagery.
