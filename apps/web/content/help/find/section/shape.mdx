## Rectangle

`Length` and `Width` are in metres. The longer of the two is the long side, whichever field it is typed in. `Long side faces` is the direction of the long side, in whole degrees clockwise from north.

A rectangle is the same either way round, so a direction and its opposite give the same roof. `Faces`, under the section, names the side that looks toward the equator.

## Corners

Each row is one corner: metres `East` and `North` of the place given under `Place`, which is taken as the middle of the roof. Enter the corners in order around the roof. `Add a corner` adds a row; the bin removes one. A row that is not complete is left out, and the roof needs three corners at least.

## How it behaves

The roof is drawn as you type, once the place and the shape are complete, and `Area`, `Faces` and `Corners` follow it. The built roof is marked `Entered by hand`.
