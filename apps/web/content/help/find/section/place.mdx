`Place` shows all the while the roof is entered by hand. It holds the place a search found, or where the roof drawn on the map stood, or what you typed: correct it here.

## What to set

Search for the building in Find's first step, before `Enter the roof by hand`: the place found is used. Otherwise type the place here.

- `Latitude` and `Longitude`: in degrees, `North and east positive, to 5 decimals.` South and west are negative. A maps link or the site's survey gives both.
- Paste coordinates, such as a pair copied from a map, or a maps link into either field: the app fills both, as the search reads them. Without a map there is no search, so this is the way to paste a place.

## What it changes

The app draws the shape typed under <HelpLink k="find.section.shape">Shape</HelpLink> about this place, in metres east and north of it. Nothing is drawn until the place and the shape are both complete.

When the roof is built, the app takes its place at the middle of its outline, and works out the sun and the weather for that place. A place found by its address keeps its own name in the design while these fields hold it; another place typed here gives no name. After `Enter the roof by hand` from a roof drawn on the map, the place is where the first corner was placed, and the obstacles drawn there stay while the place does: change it, and they go.
