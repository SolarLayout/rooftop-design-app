Once the outline is closed, `Edges` is a row of Find's column: how many edges, and where the roof's long side faces. Open it to see each edge's length.

## What to set

Measure an edge on site, or take it from a drawing, and type its length into that edge's field. Leave the other edges as drawn.

- Each edge's field: its length in metres. The edges of a rectangle are named by the side they face, such as `North` or `South-east`. The edges of any other outline are `Edge 1`, `Edge 2` and so on.
- `Square the corners`: `Snaps to 90° when you are within 3°`. On at first. Keep it on for a roof with right-angled corners.

## What it changes

A typed length moves both ends of the edge along it by the same amount: the edge grows or shrinks about its middle. On a rectangle with `Square the corners` on, the edge opposite follows, so the roof stays a rectangle. The area on `Build this roof` follows the new outline.

With `Square the corners` on, a corner placed or dragged near a right angle with the edge before it snaps to the right angle. Four corners all close to right angles become an exact rectangle when the outline is closed, the first edge kept as drawn.

The row says where the long side faces: the direction its longest edge looks, on its side toward the equator. The rows of tilted modules face the edge nearest the equator of the smallest rectangle round the roof, which on an outline that is not a rectangle can be another direction: the Studio's Array step shows theirs.

## When to change it

When the imagery leans or blurs at the roof's edge, a measured length is truer than the one drawn. Turn `Square the corners` off for a roof whose corners are not right angles. A corner then still snaps to run parallel to an edge already drawn.
