## While you draw

Until the outline is closed, the column counts the corners placed, beside `Undo` and `Clear`. A roof needs three corners at least. Click the first corner again, or press Enter, to close it.

## The length of each edge

Once the outline is closed, `Edges` is a row of the column: how many edges, and where the roof faces. Open it: each edge has a field with its length in metres. The edges of a rectangle are named by the side they are on, such as `North` or `South-east`; the edges of any other outline are numbered, `Edge 1`, `Edge 2` and on.

Type a length you measured and the edge grows or shrinks about its middle: both its ends move along it by the same amount. When the outline is a rectangle and `Square the corners` is on, the edge opposite follows, so the roof stays a rectangle.

## Square the corners

On by default. While a corner is placed or dragged, it snaps to a right angle with the edge before it when it is within a few degrees of one and close to the pointer. Four corners that are all close to right angles become an exact rectangle when the outline is closed; the first edge is kept as drawn.

Turn it off for a roof whose corners are not square. A corner still snaps to run parallel to an edge already drawn.

## Where the roof faces

The row of `Edges` says it: the direction the longest edge looks, on its side toward the equator. The area and the corners are said at the head of the column.
