<Steps>
<Step title="Press each corner">
Go round the roof's edge, one corner after another. While `Square the corners` is on, a corner close to a right angle snaps to it.
<Picture src="/help/find/corners/1.jpg" alt="Three corners of a roof placed on the map, joined by two edges with their lengths" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={19} y={73.3}>The first corner</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={80.6} y={26}>The last one placed</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Close it on the first corner">
<Pointer>Click the first corner again, or press <Key>Enter</Key>. A roof needs three corners at least.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the first corner again. A roof needs three corners at least.</Touch>
<Picture clip="/help/find/corners/2.mp4" length="0:09" src="/help/find/corners/2.jpg" alt="Four corners placed; the outline is still open between the last and the first" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={19} y={73.3} ring>The first corner: press it to close the outline</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Adjust it">
Drag a corner where the roof's edge is. Press the dot in the middle of an edge to add a corner there.
<Picture src="/help/find/corners/3.jpg" alt="The closed outline, with its four corners, a dot in the middle of each edge and each edge's length" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={80.6} y={26} ring>A corner, to drag</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={50} y={26}>A middle dot: press it to add a corner</Mark>
  <Mark n={3} x={50} y={79.3}>The edge's length</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## A step too far

<Pointer>While you draw, <Key>Esc</Key> takes back the last corner. Select a corner and <Key>Delete</Key> removes it, keeping three at least; the arrow keys nudge it a tenth of a metre, a metre with <Key>Shift</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>Undo, in the toolbar, takes back the last step.</Touch>
To set an edge to a length you measured, type it in <HelpLink k="find.section.edges">Edges</HelpLink>.
