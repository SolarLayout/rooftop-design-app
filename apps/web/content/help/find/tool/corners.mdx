<Steps>
<Step title="Click each corner of the roof">
`Corners` is in hand when the outline begins.
<Pointer>Go round the roof's edge, one corner after another. Drag the map to move it between corners.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press each corner of the roof in turn. Drag the map to move it between corners.</Touch>
Each edge shows its length. A corner close to a right angle with the edge before it snaps square, while `Square the corners` is on, as it is at first.
<Picture src="/help/find/corners/1.jpg" alt="Three corners of a roof placed on the map, joined by two edges with their lengths" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={19} y={73.3}>The first corner</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={80.6} y={26}>The last one placed</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Click the first corner again">
<Pointer>Click the first corner, or press <Key>Enter</Key>, to close the outline. A roof needs at least 3 corners.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the first corner to close the outline. A roof needs at least 3 corners.</Touch>
The column's head becomes `Add the obstacles`, with the roof's area and corners.
<Picture clip="/help/find/corners/2.mp4" length="0:09" src="/help/find/corners/2.jpg" alt="Four corners placed; the outline is still open between the last and the first" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={19} y={73.3} ring>The first corner: click it to close the outline</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Drag a corner to adjust it">
<Pointer>Drag a corner onto the roof's edge. Click the dot in the middle of an edge to add a corner there, or drag the dot to add one and move it.</Pointer>
<Touch>Drag a corner onto the roof's edge: a loupe above your finger shows it close up. Press the dot in the middle of an edge to add a corner there.</Touch>
To set an edge to a length you measured, type it in <HelpLink k="find.section.edges">Edges</HelpLink>.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key> to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with it to redo. While the outline is open, <Key>Esc</Key> takes back the last corner.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` to take back the last step: beside the count of corners while the outline is open, under the obstacle kinds once it is closed.</Touch>
`Clear`, beside `Undo`, takes all corners and obstacles off to start again, and `Undo` brings them back.

## Keys

<Pointer>Click a corner to select it. <Key>Delete</Key> removes it, while the roof keeps at least 3 corners. The arrow keys nudge it a tenth of a metre, a metre with <Key>Shift</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>To take a corner off while the outline is open, press `Undo`.</Touch>
