title: Draw a building beside the roof
summary: Press each corner of a building next to the roof, close it on the first, then give its height. Its shadow can fall on the roof.

<Steps>
<Step title="Press each corner">
Choose `Building` under `Obstacles` in the column, then go round the building beside the roof, one corner after another.
<Picture src="/help/find/building/1.jpg" alt="Four corners of a building west of the roof pressed in turn" width={1178} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={8} y={34.4} ring>The first corner: press it again to close it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Close it, and give its height">
Press the first corner again; a building needs three corners at least. Its height is asked, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>: give it above the roof's surface.
<Picture src="/help/find/building/2.jpg" alt="The new building beside the roof, picked, its handle above it and its name and height under it" width={1178} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={12.9} y={18.2} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>
