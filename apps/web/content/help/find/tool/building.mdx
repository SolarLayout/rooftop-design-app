<Steps>
<Step title="Click each corner of the building">
Choose `Building` under `Obstacles` in the column.
<Pointer>Go round the building beside the roof, one corner after another.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press each corner of the building beside the roof in turn.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/building/1.jpg" alt="Four corners of a building west of the roof placed in turn" width={1244} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={4.5} y={29.1} ring>The first corner: click it again to close the outline</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Click the first corner again">
<Pointer>Click the first corner, or press <Key>Enter</Key>, to close the building's outline. A building needs at least 3 corners. <Key>Esc</Key> takes back the last corner.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the first corner to close the building's outline. A building needs at least 3 corners.</Touch>
</Step>
<Step title="Type its height above the roof">
A card beside the building asks for it, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>. Type how far it rises above the roof's surface, not its height from the ground, in `Height above the roof`. Then press `Add` or <Key>Enter</Key>.
<Picture src="/help/find/building/3.jpg" alt="The new building beside the roof, selected, its handle above it and its name and height beside it" width={1244} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={9.1} y={13} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back the last step.</Touch>
