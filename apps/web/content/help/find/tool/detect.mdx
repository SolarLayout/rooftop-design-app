---
title: Detect the building
summary: Press inside a building to have its footprint proposed as the outline, then use it and drag its corners to the roof.
kind: tool
related:
  - find.tool.corners
  - find.tool.rectangle

<Steps>
<Step title="Press inside the building">
With `Detect building` in hand, the footprints that are known show faintly on the map.
<Picture src="/help/find/detect/1.jpg" alt="The footprints of buildings shown faintly over the map" width={1120} height={640}>
  <Mark n={1} x={24.5} y={29.7} ring>Inside the building of the roof</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Use it">
Its outline is proposed, with its corners and its area.
<Picture src="/help/find/detect/2.jpg" alt="The footprint proposed as an outline, and a note with its corners, its area, and Use this outline" width={1120} height={640}>
  <Mark n={1} x={32.5} y={76.6}>`Use this outline`: take it as the roof's outline</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Check it against the roof">
A footprint is approximate: drag its corners to the roof's edge you see.
<Picture src="/help/find/detect/3.jpg" alt="The footprint taken as the roof's outline, its corners and its edges' lengths shown" width={1120} height={640}>
  <Mark n={1} x={34.5} y={13.4} ring>A corner, to drag</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

Where no footprint is known, the note says so: outline the roof <HelpLink k="find.tool.corners">by its corners</HelpLink> or <HelpLink k="find.tool.rectangle">by a rectangle</HelpLink> instead.
