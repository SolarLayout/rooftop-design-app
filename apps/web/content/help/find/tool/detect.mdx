Take the building's footprint, its outline from Overture Maps, as the roof's outline, then correct it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click inside the building">
Choose `Detect building` in the column first: the footprints that are known show faintly on the map.
<Pointer>Click inside the building of the roof.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press inside the building of the roof.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/detect/1.jpg" alt="The footprints of buildings shown faintly over the map" width={1120} height={640}>
  <Mark n={1} x={24.5} y={29.7} ring>Inside the building of the roof</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Press Use this outline">
The footprint becomes the roof's outline, closed. A note beside it says `Footprint found`, with its corners, its area and how accurate it is. Press `Use this outline` to close the note.
<Picture src="/help/find/detect/2.jpg" alt="The footprint taken as the outline, and a note with its corners, its area, and Use this outline" width={1120} height={640}>
  <Mark n={1} x={44.5} y={63.4}>`Use this outline`: keep it and close the note</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Drag the corners onto the roof">
A footprint is close, not exact: drag each corner onto the roof's edge you see, or type a length you measured in <HelpLink k="find.section.edges">Edges</HelpLink>.
<Picture src="/help/find/detect/3.jpg" alt="The footprint taken as the roof's outline, its corners and its edges' lengths shown" width={1120} height={640}>
  <Mark n={1} x={34.5} y={13.4} ring>A corner, to drag</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

Where no footprint is known, the note says `No footprint known here`: outline the roof <HelpLink k="find.tool.corners">by its corners</HelpLink> or <HelpLink k="find.tool.rectangle">as a rectangle</HelpLink> instead.

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key> to take the footprint back, and <Key>Shift</Key> with it to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Drag a corner back to undo its move.</Touch>
