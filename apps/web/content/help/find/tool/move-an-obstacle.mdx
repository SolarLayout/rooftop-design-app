<Steps>
<Step title="Select the obstacle">
<Pointer>Click it on the map, with `Box`, `Tank` or `Tree` in hand: with `Wall` or `Building`, a click places a point. Or click its row under `Obstacles` in the column.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press it on the map, with `Box`, `Tank` or `Tree` in hand: with `Wall` or `Building`, a press places a point. Or press its row under `Obstacles` in the column.</Touch>
Its corners show, and for a box or a building a round handle. Drag a corner to reshape it: a box stays a rectangle, and the one handle of a tank or a tree, at its edge, sets its size. On an obstacle small on the screen a corner takes a press on its dot alone, and on one smaller still the corners do not show, so that a drag from inside it moves it: zoom in to reshape it.

  <Mark n={1} x={33.5} y={54.3}>The obstacle, selected: its corners show</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={33.5} y={35} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Drag it into place">
<Pointer>Drag from inside it. The arrow keys nudge it a tenth of a metre, a metre with <Key>Shift</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>Drag with one finger from inside it.</Touch>
An obstacle on the roof stays on it, sliding along the edge it meets. A building beside the roof moves freely.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/2.mp4" length="0:06" src="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/2.jpg" alt="The stair room dragged up and to the right, a dashed outline where it was, and how far it has moved" width={1200} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={37.5} y={50.3} ring>Dragged</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it by its handle">
Drag the round handle round the obstacle. A box and a building turn; a tank, a tree and a wall have no handle, and only move.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/3.mp4" length="0:07" src="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/3.jpg" alt="The stair room turned by its handle, the angle turned shown beside it" width={1200} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={37.5} y={36.7} ring>The handle</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={84.2} y={36.7}>How far it has turned</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

Each obstacle's name and height stand under it, or above or beside it where an edge's length or another label takes the room. Obstacles close together, of one name and one height, such as `Box 2` to `Box 5`, share one label where theirs have no room: `Box 2–5 · 1.2 m high`. A label with no room shows once its obstacle is selected, or the map is zoomed in.

## Remove it

<Pointer>Select it and press <Key>Delete</Key>, or press the bin of its row under `Obstacles`.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the bin of its row under `Obstacles`.</Touch>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back a move or a turn.</Touch>
