<Steps>
<Step title="Pick it">
<Pointer>Click it on the map, or its row in the list of obstacles.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press it on the map, or its row in the list of obstacles.</Touch>
Its corners and, for a box or a building, its round handle appear.
<Picture src="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/1.jpg" alt="The stair room picked on the roof: its corners shown, and a round handle above it" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={30} y={60}>The obstacle, picked: its corners show</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={25.6} y={34.2} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Drag it">
Drag it where it stands. One on the roof stays on it, sliding along the edge it meets; a building beside the roof moves freely.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/2.mp4" length="0:06" src="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/2.jpg" alt="The stair room dragged up and to the right" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={30.2} y={48.5} ring>Dragged</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it by its handle">
A box or a building turns: drag its round handle round it. A tank or a wall has no handle.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/3.mp4" length="0:07" src="/help/find/move-an-obstacle/3.jpg" alt="The stair room turned by its handle, the angle turned shown beside it" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={31} y={38.2} ring>The handle</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={95.8} y={34}>How far it has turned</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Remove it

<Pointer>Pick it and press <Key>Delete</Key>, or press the bin of its row in the list.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the bin of its row in the list.</Touch>
<Pointer>A step too far: <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key> takes it back.</Pointer><Touch>A step too far: Undo, in the toolbar, takes it back.</Touch>
