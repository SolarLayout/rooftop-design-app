A tank is anything round on the roof, such as a water tank, that takes room from the modules and casts a shadow.

<Steps>
<Step title="Drag from the tank's middle to its edge">
Choose `Tank` under `Obstacles` in the column.
<Pointer>Press at the middle of the tank and drag out to its edge.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press at the middle of the tank and drag with one finger out to its edge.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/tank/1.jpg" alt="A round tank being drawn on the roof, from its middle out to its edge" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={17.8} y={39.1} ring>The tank, drawn as you drag</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Type its height">
When you let go, a card beside the tank asks for it, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>. Type it in `Height above the roof`, then press `Add` or <Key>Enter</Key>. The tank shows its name and height beside it, and stays selected: drag the handle at its edge to resize it. A small tank shows its handle once the map is zoomed in.
<Picture src="/help/find/tank/2.jpg" alt="The new tank on the roof, its name and height beside it" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={17.8} y={39.1} ring>The tank</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back the last step.</Touch>
