<Steps>
<Step title="Drag from its middle to its edge">
With `Tank` in hand, press at the tank's middle and drag out to its edge.
<Picture src="/help/find/tank/1.jpg" alt="A round tank being drawn on the roof, from its middle out to its edge" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={20} y={39.2} ring>The tank, drawn as you drag</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Give its height">
When you let go, its height is asked, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>. `Add the tank` puts it on the roof, its name and height under it.
<Picture src="/help/find/tank/2.jpg" alt="The new tank on the roof, its name and height under it" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={20} y={39.2} ring>The tank</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>
