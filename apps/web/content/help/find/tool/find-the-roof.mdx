<Steps>
<Step title="Search for the building">
<Pointer>Type an address or a place in `Address or coordinates`, the first field of the column, then press `Search` or <Key>Enter</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>Type an address or a place in `Address or coordinates`, the first field of the column, then press `Search`.</Touch>
While the field is empty, its list shows `Examples`: an address, coordinates and a maps link. Press one to put it in the field.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the place">
Press it in the list of places found. Coordinates and a maps link need no search: the list reads them back as you type, in one row marked `Coordinates`. The map moves to the place, close enough to outline the roof, and the column's head becomes `Outline the roof`.
</Step>
<Step title="Outline the roof on the map">
Choose `Corners`, `Rectangle` or `Detect building` in the column, then draw the roof's outline: <HelpLink k="find.tool.corners">by its corners</HelpLink>, <HelpLink k="find.tool.rectangle">as a rectangle</HelpLink>, or <HelpLink k="find.tool.detect">from the building's footprint</HelpLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Other ways to start

Under the search, `Use my location` takes the map to where this device is, when you are at the building. `Enter the roof by hand` asks for the roof's length and width, with no map: see <HelpLink k="find.section.shape">Shape</HelpLink>.

## What the search reads

- An address or a place: `Koregaon Park, Pune`.
- Coordinates in decimals, latitude first: `18.53621, 73.89380`.
- Degrees, minutes and seconds: `18°32′10.4″N 73°53′37.7″E`.
- A link from Google Maps, Apple Maps, Bing or OpenStreetMap that holds the place.

Coordinates that cannot be read get a note under the field that says why. When the latitude and the longitude look swapped, the list offers them the other way round, marked `Swapped`.

A place found by its address or its name gives the design the place's own name, the first part of what the list says: `Koregaon Park` of `Koregaon Park, Pune`. The design keeps it, and Present shows it. Coordinates, a link and `Use my location` give no name.

## Keys

<Pointer>In the list of places, the arrow keys move between them and <Key>Enter</Key> chooses the one marked. <Key>Esc</Key> closes the list.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press a place in the list to choose it.</Touch>

## Without a map

When the map cannot be shown, the column opens on `Enter the roof by hand`, and a note at its head says why. Type the roof's latitude and longitude in <HelpLink k="find.section.place">Place</HelpLink>, or paste coordinates or a maps link into either field.
