<Steps>
<Step title="Search for the building">
The search is the first thing in the column. <Pointer>Type an address or a place, then press `Search` or <Key>Enter</Key>.</Pointer><Touch>Type an address or a place, then press `Search`.</Touch>
While it is empty, its list shows examples: an address, coordinates and a maps link. Press one to put it in the search and see how it is read. Coordinates and a maps link are read as you paste them, under the field.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose the place">
Press it in the list. The map flies to it, close enough to outline the roof, and the column says `Outline the roof`.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The other ways to start

Under the search, `Use my location` takes the map to where this device is, when you are at the building. `Enter the roof by hand` lets you type its length and width, with no map needed.

## What the search understands

- An address or a place: `Koregaon Park, Pune`.
- Coordinates in decimals, latitude first: `18.53621, 73.89380`.
- Degrees, minutes and seconds: `18°32′10.4″N 73°53′37.7″E`.
- A link from Google Maps, Apple Maps, Bing or OpenStreetMap that holds the place.

## Without a map

When the map cannot be shown, the column opens on `Enter the roof by hand` and says why at its head. A place you type fills its <HelpLink k="find.section.place">Place</HelpLink>.
