<Steps>
<Step title="Drag across the thing">
Choose `Box` under `Obstacles` in the column, then press at one corner of the thing and drag to the opposite one.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/1.jpg" alt="A box being dragged across a corner of the roof, its four corners shown" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={15.2} y={32.8}>Where the drag began</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={22.6} y={45.5} ring>Where it ends</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Give its height">
When you let go, a card beside it asks its `Height above the roof`, in metres. `Add` waits for a height and puts the box on the roof; `Cancel` takes it off. It is named for its kind and its count, `Box 1`; rename it in the Studio.
</Step>
<Step title="It is on the roof">
It is picked: its round handle turns it, and its corners drag. Its name and height are under it.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/3.jpg" alt="The new box on the roof, picked, its handle above it and its name and height under it" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={18.9} y={16.7} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>
