---
title: Draw a box
summary: Drag across a thing on the roof, such as a stair room or a plant room, then give its height. It casts a shadow and takes room.
kind: tool

<Steps>
<Step title="Drag across the thing">
With `Box` in hand, press at one corner of it and drag to the opposite one.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/1.jpg" alt="A box being dragged across a corner of the roof, its four corners shown" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={15.2} y={32.8}>Where the drag began</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={22.6} y={45.5} ring>Where it ends</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Give its height">
When you let go, its height is asked, above the roof's surface. Its name is suggested; type another if you like.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/2.jpg" alt="The dialog Height of the box: a name suggested, the height empty, Remove and Add the box" width={976} height={580}>
  <Mark n={1} x={50.9} y={48.8}>The height, in metres</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={67.3} y={70.7}>`Add the box` waits for a height; `Remove` takes the box off</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="It is on the roof">
It is picked: its round handle turns it, and its corners drag. Its name and height are under it.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/3.jpg" alt="The new box on the roof, picked, its handle above it and its name and height under it" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={18.9} y={16.7} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>
