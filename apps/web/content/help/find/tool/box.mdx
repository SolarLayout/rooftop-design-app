A box is anything rectangular on the roof, such as a stair room or a plant room, that takes room from the modules and casts a shadow.

<Steps>
<Step title="Drag over the stair room">
Choose `Box` under `Obstacles` in the column. It is in hand once the outline is closed.
<Pointer>Press at one corner of the stair room and drag to the opposite one.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press at one corner of the stair room and drag with one finger to the opposite one.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/box/1.jpg" alt="A box being dragged on the roof, its four corners shown" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={25.2} y={33}>Where the drag began</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={32.1} y={45.4} ring>Where it ends</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Type its height above the roof">
When you let go, a card beside the box asks for it. Type it in `Height above the roof`: `Above the roof's surface, not the ground`, as its hint says. Then press `Add` or <Key>Enter</Key>. `Add` waits for a height; `Cancel` or <Key>Esc</Key> takes the box off. The app names it for its kind and its number, such as `Box 6`; rename it in the Studio.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/2.jpg" alt="The card beside the new box, its height above the roof typed in, the hint under it, and its Cancel and Add buttons" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={66.6} y={28.7}>`Height above the roof`, in metres</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={71.1} y={63} ring>`Add`, once a height is typed</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it to fit">
The new box is selected: its corners show, and a round handle above it. Drag the handle round until its edges lie on the stair room's, or drag a corner to resize it. Its name and height show beside it.
<Picture src="/help/find/box/3.jpg" alt="The new box on the roof, selected, its handle above it and its name and height beside it" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={28.6} y={16.7} ring>Its handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back the last step.</Touch>
