<Steps>
<Step title="Click inside the roof">
<Pointer>Click a clear spot inside the roof, away from its corners and its obstacles.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press a clear spot inside the roof, away from its corners and its obstacles.</Touch>
If `Wall` or `Building` is in hand, choose `Box` first: with them, a click places a point. The roof is picked up: its outline brightens, and a round handle stands off the edge that faces nearest north.
<Picture src="/help/find/move-the-roof/1.jpg" alt="The closed roof on the map, its corners and the lengths of its edges shown" width={1138} height={578}>
  <Mark n={1} x={20} y={35.8} ring>Inside the roof, on a clear spot</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Drag it into place">
<Pointer>Drag from inside the roof. The arrow keys nudge it a tenth of a metre, a metre with <Key>Shift</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>Drag with one finger from inside the roof. Two fingers move the map.</Touch>
A dashed outline shows where it was, and a label how far it has moved.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-the-roof/2.mp4" length="0:06" src="/help/find/move-the-roof/2.jpg" alt="The roof dragged to the right and down, a dashed outline where it was, and how far it has moved" width={1138} height={578}>
  <Mark n={1} x={43.7} y={54}>How far it has moved, east and south</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={10.7} y={26}>Where it was</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it by its round handle">
Drag the round handle round the roof. The label beside it says the angle turned and where the roof's long side now faces. When the edge by the handle comes to face within a few degrees of north, east, south or west, the turn snaps square to it.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-the-roof/3.mp4" length="0:07" src="/help/find/move-the-roof/3.jpg" alt="The roof turned by its handle, the angle turned and the direction its long side now faces shown beside the handle" width={1138} height={578}>
  <Mark n={1} x={43.8} y={11.4} ring>Drag the handle round</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={65.5} y={11.6}>The angle turned, and where the long side now faces</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

<Pointer>To put the roof down, click outside it, choose another tool, or press <Key>Esc</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>To put the roof down, press outside it or choose another tool.</Touch>
The design takes the roof where it stands when you press `Build this roof`.

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last move or turn, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back a move or a turn.</Touch>
