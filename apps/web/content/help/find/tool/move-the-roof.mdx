---
title: Move or turn the roof
summary: Pick the closed roof up, then drag it into place or turn it by its round handle. Its obstacles go with it.
kind: tool
related:
  - find.section.edges
  - app.section.obstacles
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Pick the roof up">
<Pointer>With `Box` or `Tank` in hand (`Box` is the tool you hold once the roof is closed), click inside the roof, away from a corner and from an obstacle. With `Wall` or `Building` in hand, a click places a point instead.</Pointer>
<Touch>With `Box` or `Tank` in hand (`Box` is the tool you hold once the roof is closed), press inside the roof, away from a corner and from an obstacle. With `Wall` or `Building` in hand, a press places a point instead.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/move-the-roof/1.jpg" alt="The closed roof on the map, its corners and the lengths of its edges shown" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={22} y={38.1} ring>Inside the roof, on a clear spot</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="It is picked">
Its outline brightens, and the round handle appears above it.
<Picture src="/help/find/move-the-roof/2.jpg" alt="The roof picked: a brighter outline, and a round handle above its top edge" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={42.2} y={10.7} ring>The round handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Drag it into place">
<Pointer>Drag inside the roof. The arrow keys nudge it a tenth of a metre, a metre with <Key>Shift</Key>.</Pointer>
<Touch>Drag with one finger inside the roof. Two fingers move the map, as always.</Touch>
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-the-roof/3.mp4" length="0:06" src="/help/find/move-the-roof/3.jpg" alt="The roof dragged to the right and down, a dashed outline where it was, and how far it has moved" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={33.7} y={52.8}>How far it has moved, east and south</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={11.6} y={27.2}>Where it was</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it by its handle">
Drag the round handle around the roof. Its obstacles turn with it.
<Picture clip="/help/find/move-the-roof/4.mp4" length="0:07" src="/help/find/move-the-roof/4.jpg" alt="The roof turned by its handle, the angle turned and the direction it now faces shown beside the handle" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={48.2} y={12.1} ring>Drag the handle round</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={88.5} y={6}>The angle turned, and where the roof now faces</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Then

Nothing in the Studio changes until you press `Build this roof`. <Pointer>A step too far: <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key> takes it back.</Pointer><Touch>A step too far: Undo, in the toolbar, takes it back.</Touch>
