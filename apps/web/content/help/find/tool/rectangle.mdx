---
title: Draw a rectangle
summary: Drag across the roof from one corner to the opposite one, then turn the rectangle by its handle to lie on the building.
kind: tool
related:
  - find.tool.corners
  - find.tool.detect
  - find.section.edges
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Drag across the roof">
<Pointer>Press at one corner of the roof and drag to the opposite one.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press at one corner of the roof and drag with one finger to the opposite one.</Touch>
The rectangle is closed as soon as you let go.
<Picture src="/help/find/rectangle/1.jpg" alt="A rectangle dragged from its top left corner to its bottom right, its handle above it" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={19} y={26}>Where the drag began</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={80.6} y={73.3} ring>Where it ends</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it to the building">
Drag the round handle above it. Near north, the turn snaps to it.
<Picture src="/help/find/rectangle/2.jpg" alt="The rectangle turned by its handle, which stands above the middle of its top edge" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={54.4} y={12.3} ring>The handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Adjust it">
Drag a corner to the roof's edge, or type a length you measured in <HelpLink k="find.section.edges">Edges</HelpLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>
