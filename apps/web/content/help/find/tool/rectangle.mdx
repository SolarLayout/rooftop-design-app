<Steps>
<Step title="Drag from one corner of the roof to the opposite one">
Choose `Rectangle` in the column first. It draws while no corner is placed: if some are, press `Clear`.
<Pointer>Press at one corner of the roof and drag to the opposite one.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press at one corner of the roof and drag with one finger to the opposite one.</Touch>
The rectangle stands square to north, and the outline is closed when you let go.
<Picture clip="/help/find/rectangle/1.mp4" length="0:05" src="/help/find/rectangle/1.jpg" alt="A rectangle dragged from its top left corner to its bottom right, its handle above it" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={19} y={26}>Where the drag began</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={80.6} y={73.3} ring>Where it ends</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Turn it by its handle to fit">
Drag the round handle above the rectangle round its middle, until the edges lie on the building's. Within a few degrees of north it snaps straight.
<Picture clip="/help/find/rectangle/2.mp4" length="0:06" src="/help/find/rectangle/2.jpg" alt="The rectangle turned by its handle, which stands above the middle of its top edge" width={1040} height={600}>
  <Mark n={1} x={54.4} y={12.3} ring>The handle, to turn it</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Correct its edges">
Type a length you measured in <HelpLink k="find.section.edges">Edges</HelpLink>: while `Square the corners` is on, the edge opposite follows and the rectangle stays one. Or drag a corner onto the roof's edge: the outline is then one of corners, and its handle goes. To turn it after that, <HelpLink k="find.tool.move-the-roof">pick the roof up</HelpLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key> to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with it to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Drag the handle back to undo a turn.</Touch>
