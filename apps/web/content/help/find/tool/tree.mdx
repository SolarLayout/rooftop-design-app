A tree is a crown on a trunk that shades the modules. Modules keep clear of its whole crown.

<Steps>
<Step title="Drag from the tree's middle to its crown's edge">
Choose `Tree` under `Obstacles` in the column.
<Pointer>Press at the middle of the tree and drag out to the edge of its crown.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press at the middle of the tree and drag with one finger out to the edge of its crown.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/tree/1.jpg" alt="A tree's crown being drawn on the roof, from its middle out to its edge" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={17} y={68.5} ring>The crown, drawn as you drag</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Type its height">
When you let go, a card beside the tree asks for it, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>. Type the height of its top in `Height above the roof`, then press `Add` or <Key>Enter</Key>. The tree shows its name and height beside it, and stays selected: drag the handle at its edge to resize its crown.
<Picture src="/help/find/tree/2.jpg" alt="The new tree on the roof, its handle at its edge, its name and height beside it" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={17} y={68.5} ring>The tree</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={20.8} y={68.5}>Its handle, to resize its crown</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

Its crown starts at the app's usual height above the roof. Where it starts higher or lower, set `Crown starts at` in the tree's card in the Studio's Roof step.

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back the last step.</Touch>
