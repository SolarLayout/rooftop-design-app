A wall is a line on the roof, straight or bent, that takes room from the modules and shades what stands behind it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click each point of the wall">
Choose `Wall` under `Obstacles` in the column.
<Pointer>Click where the wall begins, then at each turn of it.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press where the wall begins, then at each turn of it.</Touch>
<Picture src="/help/find/wall/1.jpg" alt="A wall of two points being drawn along the roof" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={22.3} y={74.8}>Where it begins</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={45.3} y={74.8} ring>The last point</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="Click the last point again">
<Pointer>Click the last point again, or press <Key>Enter</Key>, to end the wall. A wall needs at least 2 points. <Key>Esc</Key> takes back the last point.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the last point again to end the wall. A wall needs at least 2 points.</Touch>
</Step>
<Step title="Type its height">
A card beside the wall asks for it, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>. Type it in `Height above the roof`, then press `Add` or <Key>Enter</Key>. The wall shows its name and height beside it.
<Picture src="/help/find/wall/3.jpg" alt="The new wall on the roof, its name and height above it" width={1104} height={540}>
  <Mark n={1} x={33.8} y={74.8} ring>The wall</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Undo

<Pointer>Press <Key>⌘ Z</Key> or <Key>Ctrl Z</Key>, or `Undo` under the obstacle kinds, to take back the last step, and <Key>Shift</Key> with the keys to redo.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press `Undo` under the obstacle kinds to take back the last step.</Touch>
