<Steps>
<Step title="Press each point of the wall">
With `Wall` in hand, press where it begins, then at each turn of it.
<Picture src="/help/find/wall/1.jpg" alt="A wall of two points being drawn along the roof" width={1038} height={544}>
  <Mark n={1} x={23.8} y={75.2}>Where it begins</Mark>
  <Mark n={2} x={48.3} y={75.2} ring>The last point</Mark>
</Picture>
</Step>
<Step title="End it, and give its height">
<Pointer>Press the last point again, or <Key>Enter</Key>. A wall needs two points at least.</Pointer>
<Touch>Press the last point again. A wall needs two points at least.</Touch>
Its height is asked, as for a <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">box</HelpLink>.

</Step>
