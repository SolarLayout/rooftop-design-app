Find asks for the roof type once the outline is closed, and beside a roof entered by hand. The Roof step shows the same choice in short words: `Tilted rows`, `East-west`, `Sloped`.

## What to set

Keep the default, `Flat, rows facing south` (`Tilted rows`), for a flat roof. South of the equator it reads `Flat, rows facing north`.

- `Flat, rows facing east and west` (`East-west`): for a flat roof where more modules count for more than the energy of each.
- `Sloped`: for a pitched roof, with the modules laid on its surface.

## What it changes

The app lays the modules out by it.

- Tilted rows stand at the array's tilt and face the roof's edge nearest the equator. The app spaces the rows so that they do not shade each other in the <HelpLink k="studio.section.shade-free-hours">shade-free hours</HelpLink>, unless a row spacing is typed. A walkway crosses the rows after a set number of modules.
- East-west units stand back to back at a low tilt, with a gap between them only where the shade-free hours need one. A walkway comes after a set number of rows.
- Sloped modules lie at the `Roof pitch` and face the `Direction (azimuth)` of the slope, both set in <HelpLink k="studio.section.edges-and-access">Edges and access</HelpLink>. The rows follow each other with no gap for shade.

The roof type also sets the defaults of two temperatures: `Cell rise`, with which the strings are sized, is higher for flush modules; the `Heat loss factor (Uc)` in Performance is highest for tilted rows and lowest for flush modules. A sloped roof gets no rear gain from bifacial modules.

## When to change it

Choose `Sloped` when the roof is pitched. The app then removes the parapet (its height becomes 0), and gives the roof a pitch if it had none: type the real one. Choose `East-west` to compare the energy of more modules at a low tilt with that of tilted rows.
