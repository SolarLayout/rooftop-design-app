An obstacle is anything on the roof that takes room and casts shade: a box such as a stair room or a plant room, a tank, a wall, a tree, or a building beside the roof.

## What to set

Add each obstacle with its kind, where it stands, and its height above the roof surface, never above the ground. Measure the height on site: the imagery cannot give one.

- On Find, once the outline is closed: choose `Box`, `Tank`, `Wall`, `Tree` or `Building` under `Obstacles`, draw it on the map, then type its `Height above the roof` and press `Add`. The steps: <HelpLink k="find.tool.box">Add a box</HelpLink>, <HelpLink k="find.tool.tank">Add a tank</HelpLink>, <HelpLink k="find.tool.wall">Add a wall</HelpLink>, <HelpLink k="find.tool.building">Add a building beside the roof</HelpLink>. A tree is drawn as a tank is, from its middle to the edge of its crown.
- In the Roof step: under `Add one: choose its kind, then place it on the roof.`, press its kind. Press the roof where it stands, type its `Height above the roof`, then press `Add the box`, `Add the tank` and so on.

## What it changes

Each obstacle counts three times.

- No module stands on it, nor within the `Clearance` round it, set in <HelpLink k="studio.section.edges-and-access">Edges and access</HelpLink>.
- The area its shadow covers in the <HelpLink k="studio.section.shade-free-hours">shade-free hours</HelpLink> of the design day is kept free of modules, unless `Allow shaded positions` is on.
- Its shadow counts, hour by hour over the year, in the shade of every module it falls on.

A tree's crown starts at `Crown starts at`, and modules keep clear of the whole crown. A building beside the roof takes no room on it, unless its clearance reaches the roof: its shadow counts all the same.

## When to change it

When a height measured on site differs, select the obstacle in the list or on the roof, and type it in its card. When a drawing gives no height, a note over the obstacles says `The file has no height for these obstacles. The app uses 1.0 m.` Press `Review`: the first of them opens, its card saying `Not in the file. Enter the real height.`, and `Next` goes on to the next.

The parapet ends the list as `Parapet, all edges`. Its height is the <HelpLink k="studio.section.edges-and-access">`Parapet height`</HelpLink> of Edges and access.
