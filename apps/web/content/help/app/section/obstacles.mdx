## What an obstacle does

Each obstacle counts three times. No module is placed on its footprint or within the clearance around it, set under `Clearance` in <HelpLink k="studio.section.edges-and-access">Edges and access</HelpLink>. The area its shadow sweeps through the shade-free window of the design day is kept clear of modules, unless shaded positions are allowed. And its shadow is counted, hour by hour over the year, in the shading of every module it falls on.

The height is always above the roof surface. A building beside the roof is drawn with its height above the roof too.

## On Find

Once the outline is closed, draw a box, a tank, a wall or a building with the tools on the map. The imagery cannot give a height, so each asks for it as soon as it is drawn; `Remove` takes it off again. Select a row to select it on the map, or remove it with its bin.

## In the Roof step

`Add` offers a box, a tank, a wall, a tree or a building. A new one starts at the middle of the roof (a building beside it); enter its height, then `Add the <kind>` or Enter joins it to the design, and `Remove` drops it. Select a row to open its card: its name, its size, its `Height`, and where it stands in metres `East` and `North` of the middle of the roof. A box also turns by its `Rotation`, counter-clockwise. A wall and a building are tables of points. A tree is a crown on a thin trunk: `Crown starts at` is where the crown begins, and modules keep clear of the whole crown.

When the roof has a parapet, it ends the list as `Parapet, all edges`; its height is set in Edges and access. An obstacle imported from a drawing without a height gets a stand-in height and the mark `Height to review` until you type the real one.

Closed, the section is one line that names them, marked `Yours`. It opens by itself while one is selected on the roof or has a height to review.
