## The audit's issues this closes

From [`plan/v2/audit.md`](https://github.com/SolarLayout/rooftop-design-app/blob/main/plan/v2/audit.md), each read in full there (its "Direction" is what the board settled):

- **V2-52** (P2) · Help summaries that start with "The": Every topic's summary is a noun phrase with a trailing clause ("The parapet, the setback from the edges, the walkways, the clearance around obstacles and the roof's albedo.").
- **V2-83** (P1) · Help explains the engine, not the task: The topics open with what the control is ("The parapet, the setback from the edges…"), then how it behaves; the question a new user has ("what should I put here?") is answered last or not at all.
- **V2-84** (P1) · A disc on every heading: Nine discs on the Roof inspector, each the same amber circle.
- **V2-85** (P2) · The help sheet's pictures: are blank in this environment (a loading state with no words).
- **V2-86** (P2) · The tips card and "Show me how": are a third help channel beside the disc and the docs.
- **V2-87** (P2) · The help's "Related" and "In the docs": are two lists of links at the sheet's foot with the same names.

## Scope in

1. Every topic of `apps/web/content/help` (31 today, and any added by issues 2 to 10 and #220) rewritten, under the titles and with the summaries of the board's "The topics" (as the owner approves or changes them) (its list of all 31 in V2, by where their ? sits; renames: Location → Outline, Shade-free window → Shade-free hours, Day → Design day, Shading over the year → Shade over the year, Sun at the hour → The sun at the time, Modules in series → Modules a string, Draw a box/tank/wall/building → Add …), per `plan/v2/voice.md` §9: the summary one sentence under 20 words of what the thing does for the user; `What to set` first (the usual values), then `What it changes`, then `When to change it`; the screen's labels in code formatting, as the screen says them after issue 11; never "the engine"; imperatives for steps, numbered; terms defined where first used.
2. The disc quiet (issue 1) and the field's own hint carrying the one-line answer, so most users never open the sheet: each topic's first line and its field hints say the same.
3. One list at the sheet's foot, `Related`, with the docs in it (the book icon, D91); `In the docs` as a separate list removed (`HelpSheet.tsx`).
4. How-tos reached from their section's ? (the tips card gone, issue 4): Find's how-tos linked from Outline the roof's and Find the roof's ?.
5. The sheet at three sizes as the board draws it: from the right on desktop (440) and tablet (480, the whole height), from the bottom on phone; a how-to's Back to its topic; numbered steps each with a picture; the picture opened larger and the clip's Play and Pause as built (D81). A picture that does not load says so: `The picture did not load` with `Try again` (`PictureLarge.tsx`, `PictureClip.tsx`).
6. The how-tos' pictures and clips retaken from the V2 screens (`bun help:pictures`, `bun help:clips`, `docs/help-content.md`, "Pictures" and "Clips"), where the screen they show changed.
7. `bun help:gen`, the manifest committed; the help gate green.
8. `docs/help-content.md` updated to V2's rules for a topic (the order, the summary, the words).

## Rules that hold for this work

- Build to the board; tokens only (hard rule 9, the V2 tokens of D94). A missing value becomes a token first.
- Test first: the pure logic of every behaviour in "Scope in" in a `*.test.ts` that fails before the change. No new
  browser suites and none run (the owner, 2026-10-11); the screens are checked by hand at three sizes.
- Every word the user meets follows `plan/v2/voice.md`; the app's own words are explained once, as D96 says (`EXPLAIN` in
  `docs/design/mockups/build_v2_parts.py` holds the approved lines; the app keeps them in one module).
- Never a red cross, never "bankable" (hard rules 4, 5); the system voltage rules of D87 and D88 hold.
- `bun check --skip=e2e` green before every commit; `bun gen:api` after a change to the API's routes or models.
